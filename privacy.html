Privacy policy

rclone (Mac mini) is used only by its developer for personal backups.

  • It accesses only the developer's own Google Drive, through the Google Drive API, to upload, list and verify backup files.
  • It does not access any other person's data.
  • Data from Google Drive is not collected, stored elsewhere, shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings.

Last updated: 18 November 2026.
